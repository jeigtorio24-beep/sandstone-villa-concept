Questions

Asked before arriving.

Anything else, ask the guesthouse directly.

Call +44 1631 564483Email
Is breakfast included?

No. “Accommodation is let on a room only basis, we do not provide breakfast.”

What's in the rooms?

Every room has a shower en suite, a TV, a hairdryer, a hospitality tray and toiletries.

Is there a ground-floor room?

Yes: one of the two king-size bedrooms is on the ground floor. Mention it when you call or email.

Is there parking?

Limited private parking: 2 spaces, pre-bookable. Ask for one when you enquire.

Can I bring my dog?

Sorry, no pets.

Is there WiFi? Can I smoke?

WiFi is free. Sandvilla is non-smoking.

How many people can stay in 9INE?

The flat has two bedrooms and sleeps up to 4 people. Adults and children are welcome.

How do I get into 9INE?

Self check-in by lockbox. The flat also has a full kitchen.

How far is 9INE from the stations and the ferry?

The bus and train stations are 1–2 minutes' walk from the flat, and the CalMac ferry terminal is 4–5 minutes' walk.

Can I travel on without a car?

Their homepage lists easy transport: trains and buses to Glasgow, Edinburgh, Fort William and Inverness.

What about prices and dates?

Ask us: call +44 1631 564483 or email sandvilla@holidayoban.co.uk with your dates.
